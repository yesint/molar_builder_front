SDF to GROMACS

About this service
  • This service produces GROMACS topologies for small molecules, compatible with CHARMM36/CGenFF. This is an independent clean-room reimplementation of CGenFF based only on published papers and publicly available artifacts.
  • You may use the generated topologies for any purpose, including commercial use.
  • The processing price is $1 per 1,000 atoms. The fee is used to maintain the compute server.
  • No registration is needed:
    1. Upload an SDF file with one or several molecules.
    2. Pay the processing fee via PayPal.
    3. Wait until processing is finished. Do not close the tab, or your results (and money) will be lost!
    4. Download the results.
